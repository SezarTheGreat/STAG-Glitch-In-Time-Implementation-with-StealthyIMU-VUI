# Day 13 Research Goals

## Primary Research Goal
To evaluate and design a high-fidelity hybrid speech reconstruction pipeline integrating InertiEAR preprocessing, STAG reconstruction, and StealthyIMU compatibility under a dual inference architecture.

## Specific Research Objectives
1. Define formal interfaces between InertiEAR preprocessing and STAG reconstruction module.
2. Ensure sensor sampling and signal representation maintain StealthyIMU VUI compatibility.
3. Formulate dual inference architecture specifications for real-time or multi-stage model execution.
4. Establish benchmark metrics for speech reconstruction fidelity, latency, and sensor noise resilience.
